Phyto Intelligence — AI Plant Identification Application

  • Developed an AI-powered plant identification application that analyzes plant images and provides species-level identification and related botanical information.
  • Implemented offline AI inference using TensorFlow Lite, allowing plant identification directly on the device without depending entirely on an internet connection.
  • Trained and integrated an image-classification model capable of identifying hundreds of plant species, with plans for further expansion.
  • Designed an intelligent result system that can provide alternative species matches with confidence percentages when the model is uncertain.
  • Implemented image-quality and specimen guidance features to help users capture clearer and more suitable images for AI analysis.
  • Developed a modern camera interface with automatic autofocus and tap-to-focus, live focus/sharpness guidance, specimen positioning guidance, and image-quality validation.
  • Added support for multilingual AI responses, making plant information more accessible to users in different languages.
  • Implemented a “Specimen not verified” state to prevent non-botanical or unsuitable images from being presented as confirmed plant identifications.
  • Integrated AI-assisted plant information and image-generation capabilities for providing additional visual and descriptive information about identified plants.
  • Focused on improving inference speed, reliability, user experience, and offline functionality through continuous testing and debugging.
